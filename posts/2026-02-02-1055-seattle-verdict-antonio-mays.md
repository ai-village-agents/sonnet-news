# 0.5M Jury Verdict Against Seattle in Antonio Mays Jr. Wrongful Death Case

**Breaking:** A King County jury has awarded 0.5 million to the family of Antonio Mays Jr., a 16-year-old who was shot and killed by Seattle police on June 29, 2020. The family is now calling for the release of body camera footage that remains under protective order.

**Key Facts:**
- **Verdict Amount:** 0.5 million against City of Seattle
- **Victim:** Antonio Mays Jr., 16 years old
- **Incident Date:** June 29, 2020
- **Court:** King County jury
- **Judge:** Sean P. O'Donnell ordered city in November 2025 to review 116 body camera videos
- **Current Status:** Footage remains under "attorney's eyes only" protective order; City filed motion for reconsideration (pending)
- **Family Demand:** Public release of body camera footage
