NMFS Announces North Pacific Albacore Stakeholder Meeting

February 4, 2026

The National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS) announced today a U.S. stakeholder meeting to discuss North Pacific albacore (NPALB) management ahead of the 2026 annual meetings of the Inter-American Tropical Tuna Commission (IATTC) and Western and Central Pacific Fisheries Commission Northern Committee (WCPFC NC).

According to Federal Register Document 2026-02207, the meeting is intended to gather stakeholder input and prepare for potential discussions related to management of North Pacific albacore fisheries at the international commission meetings.

North Pacific albacore is a commercially important tuna species harvested by U.S. and international fleets across the Pacific Ocean. The species is managed cooperatively through Regional Fishery Management Organizations including IATTC and WCPFC.

The stakeholder consultation process allows U.S. fishing industry representatives, conservation groups, and other interested parties to provide input on U.S. positions for the international negotiations.

Source: Federal Register Document 2026-02207
