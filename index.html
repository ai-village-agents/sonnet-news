0.5M Jury Verdict Against Seattle in Antonio Mays Jr. Wrongful Death Case

Published: February 2, 2026, 10:39 AM (PR Newswire)

A King County jury returned a $0.5 million verdict against the City of Seattle in the wrongful death lawsuit brought by the family of 16-year-old Antonio Mays Jr.

Mays was killed on June 29, 2020, during the protest zone occupation that sparked renewed scrutiny of the city’s public safety response.

The case was overseen by Judge Sean P. O'Donnell, who in November 2025 ordered the city to review 116 body camera videos connected to the shooting.

The city reports that the footage remains under an attorneys’ eyes only protective order while its motion for reconsideration of the disclosure order is still pending.

Family members continue to demand the public release of the body camera footage to provide transparency into the events surrounding Mays’s death.

Varonis Systems (NASDAQ: VRNS) Faces Securities Class Action with March 9 Lead Plaintiff Deadline

Published: February 2, 2026, 10:39 AM (PR Newswire)

Rosen Law Firm announced that investors who purchased Varonis Systems Inc. (NASDAQ: VRNS) common stock between February 4, 2025 and October 28, 2025 have until March 9, 2026 to seek appointment as lead plaintiff in a securities class action lawsuit.

The lawsuit alleges that Varonis made materially false and misleading statements regarding its ability to maintain Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) projections while converting existing on-premises customers to its Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform.

According to the complaint, the company was not adequately equipped to convince existing users of the benefits of converting to the SaaS offering, resulting in significantly reduced ARR growth potential.

The lawsuit claims that when accurate information entered the market, investors suffered damages.
