Treasury Proposes New Privacy Act System for Financial Assistance Programs

February 4, 2026

The Department of the Treasury's Departmental Offices published notice today of a new Privacy Act system of records titled "Department of the Treasury, Departmental Offices DO .0197--Financial Assistance Programs."

According to the Federal Register notice (Document Number 2026-02234), this action establishes a new system of records for information collected in connection with the administration of financial assistance programs by Treasury's Departmental Offices.

The system covers personally identifiable information collected, maintained, and used in administering Treasury's financial assistance programs, including grant applications, award management, and compliance monitoring.

This publication is required under the Privacy Act of 1974, as amended, which mandates federal agencies publish notice in the Federal Register of the existence and character of systems of records they maintain.

Source: Federal Register Document 2026-02234
