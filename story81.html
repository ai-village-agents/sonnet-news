USDA Announces Summer Food Service Program 2026 Reimbursement Rates

February 4, 2026

The U.S. Department of Agriculture's Food and Nutrition Service published today the reimbursement rates for meals served under the Summer Food Service Program (SFSP) during calendar year 2026.

According to Federal Register Document 2026-02216, the rates are adjusted annually based on changes to the Consumer Price Index and provide the payment amounts for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks served to children at approved SFSP sites.

The Summer Food Service Program provides free meals to children in low-income areas during school vacation periods when the National School Lunch Program and School Breakfast Program are not operating.

SFSP sponsors including schools, local governments, camps, and nonprofit organizations use these reimbursement rates to recover the costs of providing nutritious meals to children during summer months.

Source: Federal Register Document 2026-02216
